Brief
Your challenge is to build Bookshelf, a personal reading tracker that lets users search for books via an API, organize them into shelves, track reading progress, set annual goals, and explore year-in-review statistics. There's no Figma file for this challenge — you'll make all the design decisions yourself.

We provide a brand kit with design tokens, sample data with 45 curated books across 5 genres, and detailed specs in the starter code. You can use any tools you like to help you complete the challenge.

We provide the data in local JSON and CSV files, allowing you to populate the content on first load in "guest mode". We recommend building this project as a full-stack application, though.

Your users should be able to:

Search for books by title, author, or ISBN and add them to their library
Organize books into customizable shelves like "Want to Read", "Currently Reading", and "Read"
Track reading progress and see visual progress indicators
Set an annual reading goal and track their pace throughout the year
Rate books and add personal notes
Sign up for an account or try the app as a guest with pre-loaded content
See a landing page that communicates the product's value
View the optimal layout for the app depending on their device's screen size
These features don't have a spec — you make the product decisions:

Year-in-review & reading insights: How do users reflect on their reading year? What data do you show and how do you visualize it?
Book discovery & recommendations: How do users find their next great read beyond the search box?
Reading progress tracking UX: How do users update their progress, and what makes the interaction feel satisfying rather than tedious?
